About Us & Capability

Precision surface preparation and heavy steel protection.

Founded in 2007, Kencana Painting Sdn Bhd specializes in comprehensive blasting and painting on all engineering steel works. Operating from a 6-acre facility with an auto blasting machine and more than 25,000 ft2 of covered painting sheds, we deliver uncompromised quality in surface preparation and protective coatings.

Our operations strictly adhere to the international standards including the Steel Structures Painting Council (SSPC) and the Swedish Standard Institution (SA Standard), guaranteeing long-term durability for demanding industrial environments.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class surface preparation, anti-corrosion, and fire-resistant coating solutions that safeguard critical industrial infrastructure against extreme environments.

Our Vision

To be Malaysia's premier and most trusted specialist in blasting and protective coating contractor for all kinds of steel works.
